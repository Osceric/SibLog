King of Demons

You are a demon prince. Summon hordes of demons, march them through a web of planes and portals, and fight to hold planes against rival princes. Each turn you give orders, the turn resolves, and a detailed report tells you what happened, what you know and what needs your attention next.

Play solo against computer-run NPC princes, or host and join multiplayer games online where everyone submits orders and reads results on the site.

Space Scrum

A space conquest game where every player plans at the same time. Research technology, design your own ships, build fleets, colonize planets and send them across the map; then the turn resolves for everyone at once and you read the report. Sensors, fog of war, Boosters for long trips, and battles decided by tech as much as numbers.

Play solo against NPC players, or start an online game and send your friends a seat link.
